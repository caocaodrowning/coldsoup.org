Rolling cigarettes could maybe be considered one of the most popular past times in the country I was unfortunate enough to be born in.

Or at least that is the impression I had been given in the 18 years I've spent on this earth. Smoking straights is in fact about as common as rollies, if only a bit less so, but the only person I could ever really name who smokes straights is myself.

Indeed I had never been skilled at the act of rolling tobacco, I had of course done it to begin my smoking career as its the way my father smoked, but as soon as I could I was buying straights.

Lucky strikes were my delight, but often I fled to counterfeits. I would traipse the halls of deepnet markets buying up cartons, and often end up with strange brands.

So too was my goal a few nights before, which I was filled with shock to find myself out of straights, and left with only a packet of loose tobacco.

On wednesday or thursday I believe it was, I was intent on buying a carton of richmond kings, to ease my funds for a few weeks. But in a whim as befits myself, I had spent all my funds on drugs a plenty.

And so on saturday night, or sunday morning if you prefer. I found myself forced to roll a cigarette on various mind altering substances.

I had heard the flint of a clipper lighter could somehow serve to help you pack, but couldnt find the supposed function and simply suffered as I tried and tried.

As I pathetically rolled it up and tried to seal it, my mind wandered to yu dafu's story 'nights of a spring fever' I thought of the young girl who worked at a cigarette factory.

Though it was a mundane thought without much logic, as she probably wouldnt be rolling anything by hand. And as my mind continued to wonder, I figured I should just buy a cigarette packing machine, and a pack of lucky strikes when the store opens.
