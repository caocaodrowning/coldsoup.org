I remember that some time ago I purchased a used smartwatch for 22 pounds sterling.

It was something I did for no real reason. But if I had to recall any exact reasoning, I wanted to track my sleep. But it was essentially just a mundane whim typical of myself. I surely expected to wear it and receive some kind of definitive proof of my own dysfunction, but in the end all I got was a collection of barely below average stats, in a worthless google app, and it didn't even track my sleep properly.

The last time (or maybe it was the time before) that I reread punpun, I had read it in a single sitting, and found a disgusting image of myself reflected in it more than ever, but I also found that it no longer emotionally affected in the way I had come to expect from it.

I believe I once again reread it in one sitting the next day, and found myself more emotionally affected, but I was nonetheless left disappointed, and wondered whether my increasing age had too made me increasingly aware of its flaws.

Less than three months later and the reflection only grows more vivid, but I once again found myself deeply affected by it. Perhaps because of my intoxicated state throughout the reread, but nonetheless I saw myself reflected so deeply in its pathetic protagonist that it almost drove my heart to tears.

I remember, Volume eleven of the manga, I had read squatting in a tub of cold water. See the prior four or so volumes had been read while laying in the sun, crippled by despair without cause and an excess of psychedelics. So caked in sweat, I had decided to wash it off in a uncharacteristically cold bath.

Perhaps that goal was made somewhat redundant by the fact I basically just continued to chainsmoke and read throughout, but either way.

On entering the bath I found myself reminded of my cowardice, reduced to simply squatting half deep, lacking the courage to fully submerge.

The initial goal I suppose had been to finish the manga in this cold bath, but I could hardly manage the eleventh volume, and so as soon as it ended I scurried out of the bath and returned to my room.

I felt my heart quake and shiver as I approached chapter 139, but in the end I failed to cry, and quickly moved to write this pointless diatribe upon finishing the final volume.

The final thing my mind returns to is when I undressed to enter the bath. I of course began by staring at my own reflection as is required of one self absorbed as myself, but by the time I remembered what I was doing, the bath was essentially full.

I quickly rushed to get my shorts off and move an ashtray to the side of the bath, but just as I put my toes into the water, I suddenly remembered I had forgotten to remove an article of clothing.

And so I tossed the smart watch I had recently purchased used for a bargain price far across the room, wondering who I could pass it onto to get rid of it.
