As of late I've been lying about an office job.

This office job is of course a work of complete fiction, but when a family member asks a question like 'what are you doing for work', the question leaves no space for my answer of 'nothing'.

A cruel question, such as 'what are you doing for work' comes pre loaded with the expectation that I do indeed work, and that my source of income is something besides lying about all day and collecting welfare.

Consequently, during a recent family gathering, when the aforementioned loaded question was shot my way, I had no time to dodge, and as such blurted out the fabrication which has led me to my present lying streak.

I cant say I personally enjoy lying, but it would also be incorrect to say I struggle with it, I'm frankly a less than honest man, and if I say something once I have no difficulty repeating it; so having shoveled the first heap of my metaphorical grave, I continue to claim employment at this supposed office.


The weight of unemployment is one I've carried my entire life, and yet is one I find all too unbearable, the most shameful aspect of this chronic unemployment is no doubt the empty drive I once made a show of.

I spoke of employment as early as elementary school, and yet even when my classmates and friends all had part time jobs, I had not taken even the first step towards employment, my groundless talk of future employment was in stark divide to reality in which I am, and will continue to be perpetually unemployed.

Regardless, having once lied about my employment I experienced no difficulty replicating it, and now spend the time which could just be spent making fiction reality instead pondering the technicalities of this make belief work place.

It's little things like inventing co-workers to have faux chats with or superiors to pretend i loathe loathe, I've imitated in my head the kinds of stories I oft hear from office workers like barely functioning technology or soul crushing overtime.

In a recent development I've started wearing ties around my apartment, I too have gone from waking up at generally around 16:00 to waking up at 06:00. I spend my days filling out spreadsheets with mundane information or making presentations about nothing.

At this point I've spent enough time in my make believe world that I earnestly believe I could truly make a model employee at any local business.

However... I've yet to find the motivation to approach any said business for employment.
