The time is 03:29, and I find myself sitting in the seat of an open air noodle stand, aside from an old friend of mine, lard and capsaicin stick to the hot air and irritate my pores. Ignoring the glares of the shop owner, I gleefully suck away on my new Ritz Gold cigarettes as my friend slurps away at his noodles. For some reason he's dressed in a kimono, it's a rather ugly shade of green.

"Say, why are you dressed in a kimono again?" I inquire.
"You know, I was actually educated in japan."
"You were?"
"Plenty of people were, you'd be surprised," he then paused for a moment, "not that you would know I suppose, it used to be one of those terrible habits wealthy families have."
"Well I'm not quite old enough to know about all that anyway, and you've been dodging the actual question of why you're wearing it all night."
"Anyway, the cigarette deal went well I suppose?"
"Have you seen anyone else smoking Ritz Gold's lately?"
"True enough, any issues with the dealer?"
"No, why do you ask?"
"Well I've heard he's a little overly friendly."
"That's certainly true, I had no issues with him though."
