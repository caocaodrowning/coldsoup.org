The time, I did not know. I found myself sprawled across the ground, in complete darkness. The ground was soft and warm, my body seemed to sink into it. I had fallen into a deep sleep long ago, and just awoken.

As I instinctively began to sit up, I found that the furthest reaches of my being were immobile. Adrenaline began to shoot out towards them, as my centre began to palpitate like a ritualistic drum beat. Instinct dethroned logic as I tried to pull and pull and tug and tug, the effort growing more and more forceful the clearer its futility was. As my desperation quickly became more defined than my goal itself, I suddenly came to the realization I had been sinking deeper and deeper into the ground.

Every fibre of my being now ablaze with panic, I ramped up my ceaseless effort to wrestle my extremities out from whatever was restraining them, I shook my body like a madman as the ground sank deeper and deeper and my body was pulled down with it, I shook and shook and wrestled and wrestled and I felt myself moving further and further from my goal, and the fire encompassing my body grew hotter and hotter.

As I tried to pull myself up rather than just wrestling my extremities down, the fire once again grew hotter, and I became suddenly aware that it was in fact the pain of my extremities being pulled apart――my previously lean tendons had now been stretched to the point that if I freed my hands and feet, they would surely just loosely dangle from their sockets.

As realisation set in on my fate, the ground seemed to almost snap open like a popped balloon as I suddenly fell deep into a chasm, and felt the final pain of my hands tearing from my wrists, and my feet from my ankles. I landed once again, seemingly unharmed from the fall, or perhaps my attention simply had larger sensations to give focus, but as the pain buzzed through my body like an electric shock, it overwhelmed my senses so that I almost failed to even register it as pain.

My heart beating faster than time itself, I felt a shooting sensation deep within my shoulder, and I instinctually lifted my arm in an instant to soothe it. Blood quickly began to drip onto my face, reminding me that my body had been full of such a liquid, and my attention suddenly shifted to the feeling of it. Rushing down from my now surely ridiculous wrists and onto my face like a waterfall, the blood gave an almost coarse yet gelatinous sensation, like sandpaper coated in sharkfin soup. Surely it stripped away many layers of my skin as it continued to flow down and down, but coming upon another revelation, I realised the lower layer had some kind of light which was shining through my eyelids.

Quickly, I pulled my eyes open, dreading the state of my body, only to find myself face to face with a cat. I find myself lying on the ground, with my landlady's cat licking my face. I quickly pull it to the side and inspect my hands, I breathe a sigh of relief as I find that they're still connected, not only that but they seem entirely unharmed, the same is true of my feet.

I rise from the ground, and instinctually make the odd choice to pull my left arm toward me with my right, rather than just raising it. Deciding not to dwell on why I did such a thing, I check the time, 10:12.
