The time, I did not know. I found myself sprawled across the ground, in complete darkness. The ground was soft and warm, my body seemed to sink into it. I had fallen into a deep sleep long ago, and just awoken.

As I instinctively began to sit up, I found that the furthest reaches of my being were immobile. Adrenaline began to shoot out towards them, as my centre began to palpitate like a ritualistic drum beat. Instinct dethroned logic as I tried to pull and pull and tug and tug, the effort growing more and more forceful the clearer its futility was. As my desperation quickly became more defined than my goal itself, I suddenly came to the realization I had been sinking deeper and deeper into the ground.

Every fibre of my being now ablaze with panic, I ramped up my ceaseless effort to wrestle my extremities out from whatever was restraining them, I shook my body like a madman as the ground sank deeper and deeper and my body was pulled down with it, I shook and shook and wrestled and wrestled and I felt myself moving further and further from my goal, and the fire encompassing my body grew hotter and hotter.

As I tried to pull myself up rather than just wrestling my extremities down, the fire once again grew hotter, and I became suddenly aware that it was in fact the pain of my extremities being pulled apart――my previously lean tendons had now been stretched to the point that if I freed my hands and feet, they would surely just loosely dangle from their sockets.

As realisation set in on my fate, the ground seemed to almost snap open like a popped balloon as I suddenly fell deep into a chasm, and felt the final pain of my hands tearing from my wrists, and my feet from my ankles. I landed once again, seemingly unharmed from the fall, or perhaps my attention simply had larger sensations to focus on, but as the pain buzzed through my body like an electric shock, it overwhelmed my senses so that I almost failed to even register it as pain.

My heart beating faster than time itself, I felt a shooting sensation deep within my shoulder, and I instinctually lifted my arm in an instant to soothe it. Blood quickly began to drip onto my face, reminding me that my body had been full of such a liquid, and my attention suddenly shifted to the feeling of it.

Rushing down from my now surely ridiculous wrists and onto my face like a waterfall, the blood gave an almost coarse yet gelatinous sensation, like sandpaper coated in shark fin soup. Surely it stripped away many layers of my skin as it continued to flow down and down, but coming upon another revelation, I realised the lower layer had some kind of light which was shining through my eyelids. Quickly, I pulled my eyes open, dreading the state of my body, only to find a cat facing me.

I find myself lying on the ground, with my landlady's cat licking my face. I quickly pull it to the side and inspect my hands, I breathe a sigh of relief as I find that they're still connected, not only that but they seem entirely unharmed, the same is true of my feet. I rise from the ground, and instinctually make the odd choice to pull my left arm toward me with my right, rather than just raising it. Deciding not to dwell on why I did such a thing, I check the time, 10:12.

Picking up the cat and slinging him over my shoulder, I open the door to find that while I had been sleeping, my friend had made his way into my home. He's sitting at the table of the main room, and being served food by my landlady. He's smoking one of his girly cigarettes and is dressed in a loosely fitting white zhongshan, the top buttons of his jacket are unbuttoned in a rather distasteful fashion.

"Don't you have work?" I ask, catching him mid-leer.  
"Ah, you're awake, took you long enough."  
"I see, you were fired."  
"Not fired, you were pestering me about work last night, so I thought I would take you to the office," he retorts, before then adding "and my wife happened to kick me out the house this morning"  
"There it is, well you can't stay here."  
"Says who? And when did I even ask?" he says, before taking a fairly long drag of his dainty little cigarette and launching into "y'know Qiulan here is really a much better host than you ever were when you lived on your own. It's pretty terrible you would endanger her with this kind of precarious living situation, you should really just man up and marry her or find your own way," he must still be drunk.  
"Did you even sleep?"  
"Sleep is a petit-bourgeois illusion."

Lacking the immediate energy for the conversation, I turn my attention to the landlady who had been silently cooking as we spoke.  
"Hey, you really shouldn't have let him in,"  
"..."  
"Hey..." she's seemingly deep in thought, and isn't responding., "Qiulan are you listening?"  
"Oh, yes?" she eventually responds, still rather out of it.  
"You really shouldn't have let that crass man inside."  
"Is that so?" she responds in a rather troubled tone, before adding "didn't he say he was taking you to the office just now though?"  
"Well that was probably a lie."  
"No, that one was true actually," he responds from the table.  
"Well either way, you really shouldn't trouble yourself with him," I tell her, as I take a seat across from my friend, and light my own cigarette.  
"So rude, so rude," my friend says, putting out his cigarette in the ashtray.  
"Whatever, just eat your breakfast and we'll go to your office."  
"You're not eating?" my landlady asks.  
"No, I feel a little hungover, plus..." as the words begin to leave my mouth, I realize she doesn't really need to know about all that, "well it doesn't really matter. I'll make him pay for my lunch, then come back for dinner."  
"I see," she replies.  
"Hold on, I'm paying for your lunch now?"  
"Finish your breakfast already"  
"Alright, alright," he says, before quickly scarfing down the rest of his meal, and raising from his seat suddenly to make the landlady jump.  
"That isn't exactly what I meant, but it's fine too I guess," I say as I also rise from my seat.

We both walk up to the door, and I quickly open it to shove my friend out, before briefly retreating to bid my landlady goodbye. I then followed him outside and closed the door.  
"Say, what district do you work in again?" I ask.  
"Huangpu idiot, are you still asleep?"  
"In fairness, you've changed jobs at least once without mentioning it to me," I say, as we start to walk, then continuing "and I remember you worked at that grubby office in Hongkou once."  
"That's besides the point, I've worked here for like three years."  
"Has it really been that long? How have I never been to the office?"  
"It's a bad look for me to be bringing in bums all the time."  
"Are you really that upset that I rushed you out the house?"  
"Nah, not really."  
"Well, whatever then," I decide as we continue walking in the city, and our bickering dies down.

Eventually we reached the tram and rode it south, sitting in silence as the driver endlessly rang his bell.
