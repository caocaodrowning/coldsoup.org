At 23:42 Saturday June 6th, I find myself sitting in some kind of children's playground. It's a rather generic though small park with a swing set, a slide, some climbing apparatus, and a bench, I can't say I've been here before. As I sit on the wooden bench and study the intricacies of the rusted no smoking sign, I feel the rivets of my pants press into my hip bones, and clean air harass my lungs.

I began as a purely social smoker, but at some point, I found myself outright averse to clean air, and began a life of almost constant chain-smoking. In some sense this is a non-issue for me as I'm without proper work, and therefore have the freedom to smoke whenever I please. But the ever increasing taxation on cigarettes has worked to dig a deeper and deeper hole into my personal finances.

That's what led me to this park, a friend of mine heard about some dealer who sells insanely cheap cigarettes. The price is apparently not only untaxed, but what is essentially production cost. '30rmb per carton', or at least so I'm told.

23:44 is now the time projected by my digital watch. We were supposed to meet at 23:30. I guess I can't expect a guy who sells contraband cigarettes to have great timekeeping, but it's a little worrying.

I stand up to stretch my back and relieve the pain of sitting down, I was never really good at calisthenics but I become rather invested in my stretching, when suddenly a noise assails my eardrums. It's a public phone ringing, I forgot you could even call them. I would have to guess it's my connect considering there's no one else in the park, or the general vicinity for that matter.

"Hello?" I inquire into the mouthpiece, only to be met with silence.
After a few moments of silence the receiver finally spits out "You looking for The Ritz?" What the fuck is 'The Ritz'?
"The Ritz?" I question.
"You smoke?" The caller asks.
"Well yes."
"Then you're looking for The Ritz pal."
I began to question, "Wha-" before I heard a sudden click, and a dial tone; he hung up on me.

My confusion grows as I take a few steps away from the phone and start to pace in a circle. I quickly come to a stop though when I come to face a previously unregistered tree. It's a lone tree standing without any neighbours, and it's rather short too. Further investigating the tree my eyes are carried to the trunk, where I now stare intently at the texture of the bark. But as my preoccupation grows its subject moves from the grit of the bark to the fuzz of my now unfocused eyes.

My mind drains of thoughts, but is then soon woken up again by a parched feeling at the top of my throat. I try to swallow and sooth the dryness, focus returning to the derelict park, but the soreness remains. My hand unconsciously reaches for my largely empty pocket, when suddenly, I feel something coarse yet flexible slither from my nape, to the base of my skull, and around to my collarbone.

It begins to softly dig into the various muscles on my shoulder, and as my body is subtly pushed forwards, the caressing of my tendons turns into a grip, as a man suddenly pulls me back to him and loosely wraps his arm around my neck.

"Sup buddy, how many cartons you lookin' to buy?" says the rather average looking face now side to side with mine.
"Who the hell are you?" I ask, reminding myself of my surroundings.
"Didn't we just speak a few minutes ago?" he asks as I unwrap his arm from around me.
"Ah" I realise, "...so you're the guy from the phone?" I suppose he walked up while I was spacing out.
"Sure am, and I was calling out to you for a good moment. You doing alright man?" he asks, fairly polite for someone who just manhandled his client.
"Not doing too badly, could use a cigarette."
"You mean that Ritz?"
"Seriously man, what is The Ritz?"

He then proceeds to usher me over to the park bench, where he sits down, dramatically swings up a small cloth suitcase I assume he left there. And pats the spot next to him as if I'm a cat. Now sitting beside my new acquaintance he begins to rather slowly unzip the flap on the suitcase, and cast it aside to reveal stacks of cigarette cartons labelled 'The Ritz'.
"Of course, The Ritz," I conclude. "...Y'know these look kind of fancy, you sure I've got the right guy?" I ask.
"The Ritz is the best of the best," he quickly responds.
"Well I'm not exactly looking for the best of the best, what's the price per carton?"
"28rmb."
"28? My buddy told me 30."
"Well you seem like a stand-up guy."
"Right, well I'll take like three cartons for now."
"Really stocking up."
"I guess... what's twenty eight times three?" I wonder as I pull out my coin purse.
"That would be 84rmb my friend."
"Y'know this feels obscenely cheap."
"My supplier is a charitable woman."
"Woman? Actually it doesn't matter, and I'll round it up to four cartons for 112," I decide as I pick out a solitary hundred rmb bill, a five rmb bill, and then an assorted collection of coins.
"Sure thing," he acknowledges.

I hand him my haphazard combination of cash, and he glances at it for a moment. Before just dropping it in the suitcase, and tossing out four cartons of cigarettes wrapped in plastic onto my lap. I quickly unwrap a carton to retrieve a pack, digging my nails into the loosely glued flap and lightly banging the foot of the carton. I then pry out a pack and carefully twirl my hand around the pack, tearing off the plastic strip. Before finally retrieving my a cigarette, and lighting it.

My throat is stained and eased by tar, as I turn my head to the man and ask, "you want a smoke?" only to be met with empty space.
"Nah I don't smoke man," he responds from a distance. I turn my head to follow his voice, spotting him atop a slide eating from a bag of potato chips, where did he get those from? or actually when did he even move up there?
"Alright then, any more direct way I can contact you to buy more?"
"Man, we'll cross that bridge when we come to it y'know," he retorts as he slowly slides down the trough of the metal slide in a terribly erratic momentum.
"Sure I guess," I resign. Having reached the bottom of the slide he sits for a moment, before shooting to his feet and walking back towards me.
"Well anyway, I've got more appointments, off to bed for you."
"Bed?"
"Well you seem pretty out of it from my perspective."
"Ah right, nah I got people to go see probably"
"Sure sure, seeya then," he concludes before then marching off into the darkness of the night. Nice guy I guess.

It's now 23:54, pretty sure I was meant to meet someone at midnight. I should get going.
