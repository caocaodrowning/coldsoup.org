At 23:42 Saturday June 6th, I find myself sitting in some kind of children's playground. The rivets of my pants press into my hip bones, as I sit on a wooden bench and feel the clean air harass my lungs.

I began as a purely social smoker, but at some point, I found myself outright averse to clean air, and began a life of almost constant chain-smoking.

In some sense this is a non-issue for me as I'm without work, and therefore have the freedom to smoke whenever I please. But the ever increasing taxation on cigarettes has worked to dig a deeper and deeper hole into my personal finances.

That's what led me to this park, a friend of mine heard about some dealer who sells insanely cheap cigarettes. The price is apparently not only untaxed, but what is essentially production cost. '30rmb per carton', or at least so I'm told.

23:43 is the time etched into my digital watch, We were supposed to meet at 23:30. I guess I cant expect a guy who sells contraband cigarettes to have great time keeping, but it's a little worrying.

I stand up to stretch my back and relieve the pain of sitting down, before a noise suddenly assails me. A public phone ringing, I forget you can even call them. I would have to guess it's my connect considering theres no one else in the park, or the general vicinity for that matter.

"Hello?" I inquire into the mouthpiece, only to be met with silence. though the faint sound of someone breathing can be heard.
"You looking for The Ritz?" the receiver finally spits out, what the fuck is 'The Ritz'?
"The Ritz?" I question.
"You smoke?"
"Well yes," I reply.
"Then you're looking for The Ritz pal."
I began to question, "Wha-" before I heard a sudden click, and a dial tone; he hung up on me.

My concern over the strangeness of this deal makes itself known at the pit of my stomach. I intently focus on the fuzz of my unfocused vision for a few moments. When suddenly, I feel something coarse yet flexible slither from my nape, to the base of my skull, and up to my cranium.

It begins to softly dig through my hair, and as my neck is subtly pushed forwards, the caressing of my scalp turns into a grip, and a man suddenly pulls me back to him.
