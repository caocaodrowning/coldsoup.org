The time is 02:03.

And I've been staring at this clock in the bottom right of my screen, for three minutes, trying to prove something. Indeed I have found within this clock the desperate desperate desperate need to prove something.

As I continue to stare at it my gaze grows sharper. I stare so deeply and so intently that at its apex I feel for a second as though in my eye a blood vessel has popped. And blood has began to shoot out onto my monitor and everything else within eyeshot.

But upon collecting myself though I realize it was simply an involuntary blink, and a rush of moisture sears through my ever dry eyes.

Whatever it was I needed to prove has slipped my mind entirely.

I readjust back down to my seat and pull my neck to the right in an attempt to stretch it out, my extended staring contest with the monitor had left my already sore neck in an even worse state. It cracks once or twice, but the issue is really the muscles rather than joints, so I accept the continued discomfort and soothe my lungs with a deep breath.
