Totono / Kimi to Kanojo to Kanojo no Koi isnt quite my second favourite vn, but it still more or less shares second place with muvluv and Chaos;Head.

And I would still generally rank it above them if I had to, as its definitely quite a large influence with the way it handles its meta elements, most clear probably on async, though as far as it's currently progressed, Chaos;Head probably actually seems like a bigger influence.

On the topic of the metanarrative actually, I am willing to bet that Shuusaku would probably make it onto my consumption page were it translated, but alas there doesnt seem to be much public interest in translating it in spite of its influence.

I first read totono about a day before I started muvluv, and a month before I finished subahibi, I'm a rather fast reader. I had vowed to write a video about dating sims about a year and a half prior after watching ACTION BUTTON REVIEWS Tokimeki Memorial another year or so prior, and then playing Love Plus on the Nintendo DS

I'm quite an easily scared person existentially speaking, and so that particularly game really gave me a lot to talk about. After speaking to a friend of mine at the time who was into making youtube videos he said I should make a script about it, by the time I played totono we werent really on speaking terms anymore, but I was still oddly determined to write that script, which is part of what lead me to playing totono and muvluv, but by the time I had finished muvluv alternative I was just invested in the medium.

Anyway, play totono right now, its pretty short, just use nyaa or something if you must.
