Crime and Punishment is actually my favourite book, though Dazai still remains my favourite writer.

I first read Crime and Punishment when I was probably like thirteen, in the summer, starting about thirty minutes after reading Notes from Underground (likely the clearer influence). I can't say exactly what I like so much about the book, but probably Raskolnikovs extreme neuroticism and anxiety.

There isnt really an interesting story for this book, so I'll say that my favourite segment of the book is probably Raskolnikov dealing with his bloody socks. Reading it for the first time it was I believe some time in the late evening, and I was lying on my side in a caravan. I had to come away with my parents as they were working on some event or another, and I was made to sleep on a rather pathetically small sort of bed / couch which hardly fit even my pre growth stature (not that I'm much taller now).

I had already consumed quite a lot of media probably too advanced for my age, but reading that segment of Crime and Punishment was probably the first time I became truly entranced in reading in such a way.\ And as it happens I believe I actually also watched The Tatami Galaxy the same summer, a very formative time indeed.
