All About Lily Chou Chou is my favourite movie.

I first watched it some time in 2023, and around that time I fancied myself an artist of some kind, but I would say I was quite creatively bankrupt. I had first watched Bakemonogatari a year or two prior, and was oddly fixated on Re: Cutey Honey, but largely just relied on those two and miami vice (which a friend of mine around the time was fixated on) as far as influences went.

So watching All About Lily Chou Chou opened me up to watching a lot of other asian movies which influenced me a lot like Fallen Angels or Love and Pop as well as leading me to get back into anime and watch stuff like Aku no Hana or finishing the Monogatari Series, which probably also opened me up to reading more visual novels.

All in all I think Lily Chou Chou's influence on me is probably less visible than a lot of other works, but it does still mean a lot to me, and probably make me the person I am today, for better or for worse.
