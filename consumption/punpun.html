Punpun is mayhaps my favourite piece of media in general, it fills me with despair.

I've read it some ten times, the first of which I was only around eleven or twelve, my phone had broken and so my only actual access to the internet was an eReader with an archaic web browser on it which I used to download manga pdf's from archive.org. I believe I read Devilman and Violence Jack first before then branching out into stuff like I Am a Hero, Punpun, and later Vagabond.

I was basically around the age of punpun at the start of the story on my first reading, though mentally aged up a few years for various reasons, and I've strangely seen myself grow to resemble punpun more and more as time passed, though I can say that my reading it doesn't have much to do with it.
