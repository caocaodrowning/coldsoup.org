I recall that the first time I did lsd I disliked the experience.

My curse of regular dissociation meant that even upon quite strong ego dissolution, I came to basically no realizations that werent already quite obvious.

However during hour seven or so, I did come across the random urge to rewatch Welcome to the NHK. So whilst struggling to keep my ungated senses focused on the screen, I started to write in my head a sort of gonzo style review of the show. By the end of the first episode I had gotten a few paragraphs in, but unfortunately also forgot how it had started, and so soon ceased my viewing of NHK, only to then remember the relevance of psychadelics within the actual novel itself when speaking to a friend.

So In the cold late-winter morning of my poorly insulated room I made a position in my bed where I could lie in relative comfort, and read most of the novel in one sittin. Struck by the relative similarity between me and the protagonist I became more and more absorbed in reading, though at this time I hadn't been a shut-in for years, and I was and am essentially just a freeter.

Either way, it perhaps ended up being a formative experience, but I couldnt really say.
