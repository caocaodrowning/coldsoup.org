This is almost certainly my most objectionable choice for favourite media.

But thats largely due to the fact that public opinion of Euphoria is mostly defined by the terrible ova, and its position among vapid anitubers as "The most disturbing anime / vn". Euphoria is certainly full of vile sexual content, and I'm not personally fond of it, but I also feel no need to actually defend my love for the game. Euphoria is a classic vn among those know.

I first played Euphoria in a position not entirely unlike when I read Dostoevsky, it was 2025 and I had been dragged out to some worthless music festival my parents were working at. And with me I had nothing but my phone, three shirts, one pair of shorts, two pairs of trousers, one jacket, one hoodie, one pair of docs, a bizarrely large tent, an external hard drive full of pirated media, two weeks on the calender, and no desire to do anything in particular.

I was of course sleeping in a tent, but one could hardly call it camping seeing as I had power in said tent, and a washing machine within pissing distance. So the night of my arrival I stared up at the night sky, unpolluted by light, cursed the stars, and decided to read Euphoria.

The precise reason I even had it downloaded it lost to me, but it was sort of a perverse joke, just the concept of playing something like euphoria in a field. And for the first few routes I mostly meandered between psychological fascination and boredom at the constant H Scenes, but upon reaching the nemu route my opinion had from from somewhat varying to a full on bipolar chart, as far as the last hour I was almost sure that clockup had just fucked up a potentially good story. But in the end it was somehow among my favourite vn's.

I'm unsure I could exactly recommend anyone plays Euphoria, but clockup is in some abstract sense probably my favourite vn dev, and if you have the stomache for it I would tell you to go right ahead.
