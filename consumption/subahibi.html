Looking Glass Insects is my favourite written work.

And the other early chapters of Subahibi follow closely behind, but although I would still call Subahibi my favourite vn, the Jabberwocky chapters did honestly lose me a bit. Looking Glass Insects though remains my favourite written work, and I would strongly recommend anyone else who is fixated on despair to read subahibi for it alone.

I recall the first time I read Looking Glass Insects was actually on canadian thanksgiving in 2024, and so since my mother is canadian she cooked up a bunch of food for it, but I was cooped up in my room reading subahibi. I had read Tsui no Sora first as I would recommend anyone else do, and so going into Looking Glass Insects I figured I already knew what was going to happen, seeing as It's my Own Invention was largely similar to Mamiya's original route. However this would not turn out to be the case, and to add on to my strife I had mistakenly read the alternative happy ending first, I wont spoil subahibi but suffice it to say the actual ending fucked me up like crazy.

And by that I dont mean I cried, I am unfortunately not the type to cry, in general media will often send me into a depressive spiral but it will not often affect my emotions in such a direct way, however I suppose you could say Looking Glass Insects was an exception as I was essentially sent into a catatonic state in which I sat on my floor for like 30 minutes staring into the abyss, before then writing some random piece.

Regardless, I was soon dragged out of my room by my parents so I could eat pierogi and ham while trying not to look depressed.
