I cant quite recall what originally drove me to read Kitakawa, or even where I heard of it. But I read it in one sitting I believe, or more realistically I was lying down――either way, I read it quite quickly and initially felt rather mixed about it.

I more or less just felt that large stretches of the manga were kind of middling and then the ending was very good, frankly an option that I still hold, but I'm such a person that a good enough ending can make something my second favourite manga.

Regardless of some fairly blatant writing flaws, the final arc of the manga is just so good that my score for kitakawa has gradually grown from an eight, to a nine, to a ten, and it served as a considerable influence on a certain work of mine.
