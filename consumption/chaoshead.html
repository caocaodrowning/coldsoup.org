Interestingly I actually first read Chaos;Head between like four countries.

I was born in wales, and until 2023 had never actually left the island, so in 2024 my parents decided to take my on some trip to Canada, Mexico, and the USA. Though that said I had to lend them some money I got from family during it as they frankly couldnt afford it, and one could less generously interpret it as visiting our family in Canada, my father meeting up with work connects in the US, and my parents wanting to holiday in Mexico. But they took me with them either way, and so during the trip whilst trying to figure out a way to entertain myself, I started looking for stuff to do on my phone.

I first tried to play actual games, but that soon proved to not work very well. And so next I thought up the idea to read a visual novel, this proved to work much better on my phone, and so I first decided to read Steins;Gate using a switch emulator. Being a fan of the anime I figured I may as well try reading the source material, until then discovering it was actually part of a series.

Such is the all in all rather mundane story of how I came to read Chaos;Head, It remained my favourite vn for a fair few months until it was dethroned by totono, and that by subahibi. At this point I would probably rank it around fourth, but Takumi's narration remains a fairly large influence on me, and I would certainly recommend it to anyone being the only non eroge visual novel on my list.
