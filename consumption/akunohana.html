I prefer the Aku no Hana manga to the anime, by a fairly wide margin actually――that said I cant particularly recall the manga as well as I've only read it once and it was a few yaers ago, and of course it's longer and has some of the better parts of the story.

But insofar as whats adapted, the anime is certainly superior. If I had to say when I first watched it I would probably guess maybe july of 2023, it's another example where I cant quite recall where I heard about it, although I already vaguely knew of the manga and Shuzo Oshimi.

Regardless of where I heard of it though, I ended up seeing the classroom scene, and whether it was the direction itself, the Asa Chang & Junray ost, or maybe just the Sade sample, I was captivated. I immediately flung my mouse towards a new tab and started downloading the show, and probabbly around five hours later I had a new show among my favourites, and also immense dissapointment at the lack of taste among anime fans and the lack of a second season.

In some sense it's a little cruel to recommend something that only adapts like a third of the story. But in my opinion something doesn't even really need a real end, chapter 364 of Berserk is kind of a great ending in my opinion, and Aku no Hana is certainly worth watching.

It might give some people blueballs, but you can always read the manga.
